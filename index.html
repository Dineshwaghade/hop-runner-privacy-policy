Privacy Policy — Hop Runner

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Overview

Hop Runner ("the app", "we", "us") is a simple endless-runner game. This policy explains what information the app handles and how, so you can decide whether you're comfortable playing.

Information We Collect Directly

The app itself does not require an account, does not ask you to enter any personal information, and does not send your gameplay data to us or to any server we operate. The only data the app stores is your best score, saved locally on your device (using standard on-device storage). This never leaves your device and is deleted automatically if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Hop Runner shows ads through Google AdMob to support free play. To serve and measure ads, Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process information such as your advertising ID, device information, approximate location, and ad interaction data, and may use cookies or similar technologies. We do not control this collection directly — it's governed by Google's own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from partner sites or apps

You can limit ad personalization through your device settings (for example, on Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization, or by resetting your advertising ID).

Permissions

The app requests internet access, which is required solely to load ads. It does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, location, storage, or any other sensitive device permission.

Children's Privacy

Hop Runner is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below so we can address it.

Data Security

Since the app does not collect or transmit personal data to us, there is no server-side account data of yours for us to secure. Data stored locally on your device (your best score) is protected by your device's own operating system security.

Your Choices

  • You can reset your locally stored best score at any time from the app's Settings screen.
  • You can opt out of personalized ads through your device's ad settings.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all data the app stored on your device.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time — for example, if we add a new feature or advertising partner. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
